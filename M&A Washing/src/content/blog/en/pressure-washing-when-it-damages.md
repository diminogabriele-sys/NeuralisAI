High-pressure water is a powerful tool. Used well, it removes years of dirt in minutes; used badly, it can erode concrete, chip stone and strip render.

## The three variables that matter

1. **Pressure.** Maximum is not always needed. On render and soft stone we work at low pressure and let a specific detergent do the work.
2. **Distance.** Holding the lance too close concentrates the force on a few centimetres. It is the most common DIY mistake.
3. **Nozzle.** A pencil jet is only useful in special cases. Large surfaces call for fan nozzles, rotary nozzles or surface cleaners.

## Surfaces that need care

- **Render and exterior paint**: pressure can strip them. Detergents and a gentle rinse work better.
- **Soft stone and marble**: high pressure can open the pores and make the stone more absorbent.
- **Sand-filled joints**: wash with an angled jet, then refill.
- **Wood**: marks easily and needs low pressure.

## What we do before every job

Before starting we always **test on a hidden spot**. That way we choose pressure and nozzle on the real material, not from a chart. If a surface is not suitable for pressure washing we say so straight away and suggest an alternative.
